A study note connecting stochastic division times to population growth and cell-size homeostasis, with step-by-step derivations, experimental figures, technical appendices, and discussion problems.

**Suggested route:** Sections 0–2 introduce the tension between the growth advantage of independent noise and size homeostasis. Sections 3–4 connect temporal correlations to population growth. Section 5 gives a short comparison of different growth rates at the same lifetime distribution. Section 6 derives how size feedback in the adder generates correlations that cancel accumulated timing fluctuations. Sections 7–8 discuss spectra and oscillations, and Section 9 draws the results together. The core derivation follows Sections 0–4 into Section 6; Section 5 provides an intuitive example, with the full Markov-model calculations in Appendix E.
