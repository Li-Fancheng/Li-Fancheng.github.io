A study note connecting stochastic division times to population growth and cell-size homeostasis, with step-by-step derivations, experimental figures, technical appendices, and discussion problems.

**Suggested route:** Sections 0–2 introduce the tension between the growth advantage of independent noise and size homeostasis. Section 3 extends Euler–Lotka to correlated division histories, and Section 4 relates growth to fluctuations of accumulated division time. Section 5 uses a Markov model to show how different temporal correlations produce different growth rates at the same single-generation lifetime distribution. Section 6 derives how the adder generates correlations that cancel accumulated timing fluctuations. Sections 7–8 discuss spectra and oscillations, and Section 9 draws the results together. For the central size-control argument, read Sections 0–4 before Section 6; Section 5 is an optional worked example.
